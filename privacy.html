PaisaTrack Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 November 2026. Publisher: TwiSon Studio ("we"). Contact: sonu.shah99098@gmail.com

What PaisaTrack is

PaisaTrack is a personal expense tracker. Your ledger is stored only on your device, in an encrypted local database. We run no servers for your data and there is no account or sign-up.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store your transactions, accounts, balances, contacts, location or identity. We use no analytics and no crash-reporting service. The app does not read your SMS or your notifications. If you paste a message, it is parsed on your device only.

Data on your device

Your entries stay on your device. Any CSV, PDF or backup file you choose to create stays on your device or wherever you save it.

Security

The local database is encrypted (SQLCipher), and its key is kept in Android's secure storage on your device. Backups you create are encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2-SHA256). We cannot recover a lost backup password. CSV and PDF exports are not encrypted, so save them somewhere you trust. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Biometric or device lock authentication is handled by Android; PaisaTrack never receives your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Your data > Delete all local data to erase your records, preferences and the encryption key; this cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Files you exported or backed up are separate copies and must be deleted by you. Flash storage may retain erased blocks until the OS overwrites them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and device and app information to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Google, not us, controls and retains this ad data under its own policies, and we cannot access or delete it for you. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we show a consent form first. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options, which appears when your region requires it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy. Your ledger data is never shared with advertisers.

Children

PaisaTrack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: you control it directly in the app. For ad data held by Google, use the ad controls above or contact Google. You can also write to us at the address below with any privacy question.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
