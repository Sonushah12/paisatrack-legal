PaisaTrack Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 November 2026. Publisher: TwiSon Studio ("we"). Contact: sonu.shah99098@gmail.com

What PaisaTrack is

PaisaTrack is a personal expense tracker. Your ledger is stored only on your device, in an encrypted local database. We run no servers for your data and there is no account or sign-up.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, receive or store your transactions, accounts, balances, contacts, location or identity. We use no analytics and no crash-reporting service. The app does not read your SMS or your notifications. If you paste a message, it is parsed on your device only.

Data on your device

Your entries, and any CSV, PDF or encrypted backup files you choose to create, stay on your device or wherever you save them. Biometric or device lock authentication is handled by Android; PaisaTrack never receives your biometric data. Uninstalling the app deletes its data. Keep your backup password safe; we cannot recover it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address and device and app information to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy. Your ledger data is never shared with advertisers.

Children

PaisaTrack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For ad data, use the controls above or contact Google.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
